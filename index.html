About Me

      
A quick rundown of who's behind the terminal.

      

        

          
I'm a Computer Science student at Africa Vision University in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire, currently interning as a full-stack engineer at Atihmam Conseils. Alongside shipping product code, I've been building a parallel track in cybersecurity — working through TryHackMe rooms, completing a Security+ bootcamp, and practicing the enumeration-to-privilege-escalation workflow on deliberately vulnerable machines.
